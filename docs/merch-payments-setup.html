Merch Payments Setup

A one-time, ~10-minute setup so merchandise can take electronic payments on the co-op's PayPal account — without the merch manager's personal Venmo, and without adding to the treasurer's plate.

The idea in one paragraph

We create a named PayPal payment link — "R&W Merch" — on the co-op's existing PayPal business account, print its QR code for the merch table, and give the merch manager her own limited login to that account so she can watch merch payments come in. Every merch payment arrives self-labeled "R&W Merch," so the treasurer can ignore them day to day. The members portal remains the ledger: the manager records each sale there (cash, check, or PayPal), and that's the record of truth.

Treasurer setup Treasurer

  1. Create the payment link. Sign in at paypal.com (business account) → Pay & Get Paid → Payment links and buttons (sometimes labeled "PayPal.Me" or "Create payment link"). Create a new link named R&W Merch. Leave the amount blank/flexible so the buyer types the total the manager tells them. If it offers "let the payer add a note," turn that on — buyers can write what they bought.
    The homepage donate button already uses one of these links, so this is the same feature you've used before — just a second, named link.
  2. Print the QR code. Every payment link has a QR code option (on the web: the link's "Share" / "QR code" button; in the PayPal Business app: Get paid → QR code). Download it, print it letter-size, and put it in a stand-up sign holder for the merch table. Print a second copy — they walk off.
  3. Add the merch manager as a limited user. Gear icon → Account Settings → Account access → Manage users → Add user. Enter her name and email; grant only view transactions and issue refunds (skip withdrawals, bank/card changes, and account settings). She gets an invite email and creates her own password — she never sees yours.
  4. Send Erin two things. The payment-link URL (looks like paypal.com/ncp/payment/… or paypal.me/…) and the QR image file. Erin wires them into the portal so the manager can show the QR from her phone and web-form buyers can pay ahead from their confirmation email.

Merch manager Merch Manager

  1. Accept the PayPal invite and sign in once so it's ready before the first event.
  2. Do one test payment — scan the QR with your own phone, pay $1, watch it appear under your login, then refund it. Now you know exactly what buyers see.
  3. At the table: Quick Sale in the portal → tap the items → tell the buyer the total → they scan and pay (or hand you cash/check) → tap the payment method → Record. Inventory updates itself. That's the entire process.

Good to know

  • Buyers without a PayPal account: PayPal is finishing an account-side fix ("guest checkout") that lets people pay a link by card with no PayPal account. Until it lands, card-only buyers can pay cash/check or pay at pickup — the portal already says so.
  • Fees: PayPal takes its normal fee on each payment (roughly 2–3%). Merch prices already absorb this; nothing to configure.
  • Refunds: the manager can issue them from her limited login; no need to involve the treasurer.
  • Old Venmo payments: stay recorded as history in the portal. Nothing new goes to Venmo.
  • Reporting: if anyone ever needs merch totals, it's one report in the portal (payments by method and date). The PayPal activity filtered to "R&W Merch" is the cross-check.
