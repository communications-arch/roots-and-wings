# The Newcomer

**Run me when:** onboarding, waivers, registration, quickstart, empty states,
or any first-run surface changes.

**Who:** A family that joined mid-season two weeks ago. Knows nothing about
co-op jargon ("grove"? "session"? "liaison"?). Nervous about doing something
wrong. Reads everything, clicks slowly, uses the back button a lot.

**Login:** newbiel@ (Cuthbert family) · **Device: PHONE** (Chrome device
emulation ~390×844). Newcomers overwhelmingly arrive from an email link on
their phone.

## Journeys

1. **First sign-in → orientation.** From members.html sign-in to "I know what
   I'm supposed to do next" without outside help. Expected: the portal's own
   cards/alerts (waiver banner, To Dos, quickstart link) form a visible path;
   nothing assumes knowledge the portal hasn't given.
2. **Sign the waiver.** Follow the waiver banner end-to-end on the phone.
   Expected: completes without pinch-zooming; banner clears afterward without
   a hard refresh.
3. **Find my kids' schedule.** Locate what classes/groves the kids are in.
   Expected: reachable within ~3 taps of the top; grove names are shown with
   enough context that a newcomer can tell it's their kid's class group.
4. **Afternoon class sign-ups (if a window is open).** Pick classes for one
   kid. Expected: the alert → modal flow explains itself; rank/choice concepts
   are inferable; saving confirms visibly.

## Exploratory charter (~10 min)

Chase every "empty" surface a two-week-old family sees: cards with no data,
reports with nothing in them, features gated to roles they don't hold. An
empty state that renders as a blank box, a spinner forever, or raw "undefined"
is a defect; one that says what would be here and how to get it is a pass.
Also: rotate the phone, use browser back after every modal, tap things twice.

## UX-notes lens

Jargon without a nearby explanation; any tap target too small for a thumb;
any moment the persona would have texted a human to ask "what do I do?"
