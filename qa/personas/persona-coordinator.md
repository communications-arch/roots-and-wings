# The Coordinator

**Run me when:** workspace sections/pills, My Responsibilities role rows,
Roles & Committees descriptions, the Collaboration page, Discussion,
participation points/badges, or any "light role" plumbing changes.
(Template — run as ONE committee-role holder; note which.)

**Who:** A member with one small year-long job and no admin tool: Yearbook
Coordinator, Field Trip Coordinator, Fundraising Coordinator, Parent Social
Events Coordinator, Summer Social Events Coordinator, Gratitude/
Encouragement Leader, Archives, Public Communications, Community Liaison,
Admin/Organization. She opens the portal once a week and wants it to say,
plainly, "here is your job, here is what's waiting, here is your credit."
Her worst experience is a workspace pill with her role's name on it that
opens onto nothing.

**Login:** Gratitude/Encouragement Leader = morning@ (Joy Anderson);
Yearbook Coordinator = registern@ (Eleanor Little — also Cedars Liaison,
so expect a liaison section beside it). Every other coordinator: recast via
View-As to the holder in Roles & Committees; confirm on dev. **Device:
PHONE.**

## Journeys

1. **My role, findable.** From sign-in: My Family → My Responsibilities
   should list the role (annual block); tapping it opens the detail with the
   role description. Then Resources → Organization & Roles → find the same
   role with the same description and holder. Expected: both agree; the
   description is not the fallback "Full description lives in…" line.
2. **My workspace section.** My Workspace → the pill named for my role.
   Expected: a To Do card that either lists something real or reads "All
   caught up — nothing pending" — never a blank card, a second copy of
   another role's card (see #251), or a body stuck on "Loading…". The Shared
   section (Special Events To Do, Ways to Help, Class Development, Lending,
   Resources) renders once, not once per role.
3. **Credit.** Ways to Help → "Your year so far". Expected: the role appears
   on the "Roles:" line; the earned-points total reflects the role's tier
   (light roles seed at 30; Gratitude 20; Yearbook 41 — read the number, do
   not compute); the plant badge in the greeting matches the panel's tier.
4. **Collaboration + Discussion.** Open the Collaboration page; pick an
   event; post a Discussion message and delete it. Expected: the pill bar
   leads with events she's on; posting shows her name and (if applicable) a
   Lead/Assistant pill; delete works only on her own message.
5. **Jump in from a light seat.** Ways to Help → Committee Seats → "I'm
   interested" on an open seat, then undo. Expected: ✓ Interested toggles;
   nothing implies a commitment was made.

## Exploratory charter (~10 min)

Hold two light roles at once (View-As a multi-role family) and look for
duplication: duplicated To Do rows, duplicated Shared cards, the same
Special Events To Do under each role. Then check the role in places it
merely appears — the Directory/roster, the Roles & Committees print, the
role-holders CSV — for a spelling that differs from the workspace pill.
Community Liaison only: confirm the Comms "Set Up a Guest" waiver form's
Community Liaison option is a non-member flow and doesn't collide with the
member role's card.

## UX-notes lens

Any moment she'd ask "…and what do I actually do?"; role names that wrap
badly as pills on a phone; points shown without a one-line "why".
