# The Operations Crew

**Run me when:** the Supply Closet, special events (grid, Jump In, the
Collaboration page, event seats), Cleaning Crew Management, or the
opener/closer duties on the Coverage Board change. (Template — run as ONE
of the four seats; note which.)

**Who:** The people who make Wednesday physically happen: the Supply
Coordinator (closet + restock), the Special Events Liaison (dates, leads,
helpers, planning spaces), the Cleaning Crew Liaison (areas + rota), and
the Building Opener/Closer (keys, first in / last out). Practical, phone in
hand, no patience for a tool that hides the one list they need today.

**Login:** Special Events Liaison = mem@ (Potter). Supply Coordinator,
Cleaning Crew Liaison, Building Opener/Closer: no dev holder on record —
find the holder in Roles & Committees and recast via View-As; if unfilled,
say so in the summary and skip that seat's journeys. **Device: PHONE** for
Supply + Opener/Closer, **DESKTOP** for SEL + Cleaning.

## Journeys

1. **Supply Coordinator — closet admin.** My Workspace → Supply Closet →
   Inventory & Locations. Expected: edit mode (add/edit items, quantities,
   the ⚙ Storage Locations drawer); items flagged by members float into a
   "Needs restocking" section; the "Buy / restock supplies" To Do count
   equals that section's count and deep-links to it; clearing a flag drops
   the count without a reload.
2. **SEL — the event lifecycle.** Special Events card → Event Dates (Admin
   Calendar, special view) → propose/approve a date; Lead & Assistants
   (Roles & Committees, Special Events lens) → assign a lead and a helper.
   Expected: the event shows on the Special Events grid with those people;
   the Board card's "events staffed X/Y" moves.
3. **SEL — seats + spaces.** Open the Jump In modal as a member (View-As
   any family), take an open seat; back as SEL the "New special-event
   sign-ups" To Do counts it and the review modal lists who wants what. Then
   open that event's Collaboration space: checklist, sign-ups, day-of
   schedule, Discussion; toggle a section's binoculars (all members vs
   committee only) and confirm a plain member sees/doesn't see it.
4. **Cleaning Crew Liaison — areas + rota.** Reports & Forms → Cleaning
   Crew Management. Expected: areas with no family this session are what
   the "Assign cleaning areas" To Do counts; assign one and both the count
   and the Board card's "areas open — Session N" drop; the Ways to Help
   "Cleaning Crew" tile's open-spot count agrees.
5. **Opener/Closer — coverage.** As the holder, file an Absence Alert for a
   co-op day. Expected: the Coverage Board shows "Building Opener — unlock &
   morning set-up" (AM) or "Building Closer / Lost & Found — end of day"
   tagged **Board only**; a non-board member sees no "I'll Cover This"
   button on it; a board member does. Cancel the absence; the slot leaves.

## Exploratory charter (~10 min)

Stress the counts each seat lives by (restock flags, open areas, open
seats, staffed events) across To Do, Board card, Ways to Help tile, and the
modal itself — any two disagreeing is a defect. Try the destructive paths:
delete a closet item that's flagged, un-assign a cleaning family mid-session,
remove an event lead who has open tasks. Everything should confirm what it
will do and leave no orphaned count.

## UX-notes lens

Management modals whose "manage" mode looks identical to browse; To Do rows
that open a modal on the wrong tab/lens; anything a phone-in-hand crew
member has to pinch to tap.
