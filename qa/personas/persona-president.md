# The President

**Run me when:** anything board-wide changes — the Board section cards, Roles
& Committees, the Admin Calendar, Board Notes, the Membership report, My
Community, or role/permission plumbing. Also after any big multi-role ship
(she is the one login that should see *all* of it hang together).

**Who:** Whole-portal oversight. She holds no operating tool of her own (no
pipeline, no builder) — her job is to look across everyone else's and trust
the summaries. She notices when two numbers disagree, when a card says
"Unfilled" that shouldn't, and when a board member's tool is missing from
the Board section. She reads more than she clicks.

**Login:** president@ (Flynn-Fletcher family) · **Device: DESKTOP** (Board
section, reports). One journey repeated on the phone viewport.

## Journeys

1. **Board section, all nine cards.** Open My Workspace → Board. Expected:
   one card per board role (President, VP, Treasurer, Secretary, Membership,
   Sustaining, Communications, Cleaning Crew Liaison, Special Events Liaison)
   plus Board Notes; each shows a holder name (or an honest "Unfilled"), live
   metrics, and view chips that open (Calendar, Members, Payments, Schedules,
   Pipeline, Rota, Events & people). No card stuck on "Loading…".
2. **Counts agree.** Note the Treasurer card's "payments pending", the Comms
   card's "waivers pending", the Membership card's "in pipeline". Open the
   Membership report (Reports & Forms → Membership; read-only for her — no
   Actions column) and the Waivers view. Expected: the pills/meta in each
   report match the Board-card numbers exactly.
3. **Co-op Management.** Roles & Committees opens the whole tree; she can
   add a role, edit meta, archive/restore, assign holders, Export CSV and
   Print. Admin Calendar opens; the "Set up" badge and the "Set co-op
   calendar" To Do appear only when next year's sessions are missing.
   Facilities — Rooms opens. Expected: nothing 403s for the President.
4. **Board Notes round-trip.** Add a note, reload, remove it. Expected: it
   appears with her name + "just now"; only her own notes (and supers') show
   the remove ✕.
5. **A member's details.** From My Community, click a count → roster → a
   family; then the Directory. Expected: consistent family/kid data between
   the two, no sensitive fields (payment, allergies) on the community roster.
6. **Phone spot-check.** Repeat journey 1 on the phone viewport. Expected:
   Board cards stack cleanly; view chips remain tappable.

## Exploratory charter (~10 min)

Hunt for things she should NOT see (Comms' Admin Consoles — Permissions,
Card Help Text, Disaster Recovery Plan — are Comms-only; Merchandise is not
on her Reports card) and things she SHOULD (every board seat's Board card,
Membership read-only, both calendar doorways). Then chase absence alerts: as
the President, file an Absence Alert on the phone, and confirm the bell/board
land it on the right people (liaisons + leads + assists, and the Coverage
Board for open slots) — not on the whole membership. Two summaries showing
different numbers for the same thing is always a defect.

## UX-notes lens

Cards whose "why is this here for me" isn't obvious; read-only reports that
don't say they're read-only; anywhere she has to open a modal to learn a
number a card could have shown.
