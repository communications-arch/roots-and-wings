# The Board Desk

**Run me when:** a board chair's To Do items, Reports & Forms rows, Admin
Consoles, Member Pipeline, waivers/onboarding, or Board Notes change.
(Template — run as ONE of the four seats below; each has its own lane.)

**Who:** A working board chair with a mailbox to clear. She lives on the To
Do card and expects every count to be a doorway that lands on exactly the
rows it counted. Seats: **Communications** = erinb@ (Padme Amidala) ·
**Membership** = membership@ (Heeler) · **Secretary** = secretary@ (Miller)
· **Sustaining** = averyl@ (Simpson). Note the seat in the sweep summary.

**Login:** the seat's account above · **Device: DESKTOP.** (Comms only:
repeat journey 1 on the phone viewport — she gets pinged there.)

## Journeys

1. **To Do → doorway → count.** For every visible To Do item, click through
   and count the rows in the modal that opens. Expected: the number on the
   item equals the rows shown, and items hide at zero (card collapses to
   "All caught up"). Per seat, the items are:
   - *Membership:* Approve enrollment changes · New Inquiries · Tour Requests
     · Scheduled Tours (with the glance list) · Send Registration Link ·
     Awaiting Registration · Set morning classes.
   - *Communications:* Member Onboarding · Pending Waivers · Resent Waivers ·
     Set up R&W sign-ins · Confirm role holders · Review & update the
     Membership Handbook · Notify non-returning members · Remove Google
     accounts · Refresh the printed DR binder (✓ Done chips stamp and stay).
   - *Sustaining:* New special-event sign-ups (shared with the SEL).
   - *Secretary:* none wired — the card should say so, not sit blank.
2. **Reports & Forms.** Open every row. Expected — *Membership:* Member
   Pipeline, Grove Builder, Membership, Send Registration Form. *Comms:*
   Waivers Report, Membership, Merchandise & Orders, Send Waiver, Set Up a
   Guest. *Secretary / Sustaining:* Membership only (read-only — no Actions
   column). Each opens with real data or an honest empty state.
3. **Seat-specific tool.** *Membership:* walk one family through the
   Pipeline (inquiry → tour → registration link) and open the Grove Builder.
   *Comms:* Admin Consoles — Permissions (grant a capability, watch the
   workspace re-derive), Card Help Text (edit one "?" text, reset it),
   Disaster Recovery Plan (opens, prints), Facilities — Rooms. *Secretary:*
   Admin Calendar from the Board card + Board Notes add/remove. *Sustaining:*
   Board Notes + a Collaboration space's committee-only sections (greyed
   binoculars) are visible to her.
4. **Board card for my seat.** Board section → my card. Expected: my name
   as holder; metrics match my To Do counts (Comms: members to onboard /
   waivers pending; Membership: in pipeline / awaiting registration).

## Exploratory charter (~10 min)

Cross seats: does anything from another chair's lane leak into mine (Comms
seeing the Tour Pipeline, Secretary seeing Actions)? Do the same counts
disagree between To Do, Board card, and report pills? Comms only: the
public bugs.html "Bugs & Ideas" page and the bell's "Send a test" push
footer — confirm dev says "Not available on the dev site" rather than
erroring.

## UX-notes lens

To Do labels that don't say what clicking will do; ✓ Done chips with no
hint of when the reminder returns; report rows whose title differs from the
modal title they open.
