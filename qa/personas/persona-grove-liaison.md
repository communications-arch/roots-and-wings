# The Grove Liaison

**Run me when:** morning classes, grove placement, the My Grove card,
Snack List, Shared Facilities, the Class Builder, classmates/rosters, or
liaison To Dos change. (Template ×9 — one per grove; run as any ONE.)

**Who:** A parent volunteer who runs one morning grove for the year: knows
every kid's allergy, who's teaching next session, whether the kitchen is
booked, and who's bringing snack. She uses the portal on Tuesday night to
prep for Wednesday. Her worst bug is a roster or teacher line that is
quietly wrong — she'll act on it in a room full of kids.

**Login:** Saplings = waterg@ (Mathews) · Willows = crtest-sanders@ (Ming
Lee) · Maples (+ACL) = afternoon@ (Park) · Cedars (+Yearbook) = registern@
(Eleanor Little). Greenhouse, Sassafras, Oaks, Birch, Pigeons: no dev
holder on record — recast via View-As to whoever holds that grove's Liaison
role in Roles & Committees; confirm on dev and note it. **Device: DESKTOP**
(prep) + journey 3 on the phone (in the room).

## Journeys

1. **My Grove card truth.** My Workspace → the liaison section → My Grove.
   Walk S1–S5. Expected: grove icon + name in grove color; per session the
   published class with Teaching / Assisting / room (or an honest "No
   morning class published for Session N yet"); the roster header's kid
   count and age span match the kids listed; allergies in red; "+ Note"
   saves and survives reload. Multi-grove logins (Maples/Cedars seats) show
   each grove separately with no duplicated "Loading…" body.
2. **Book a Shared Facility.** From the card's "Book a Shared Facility"
   (Kitchen, Kitchen Annex, Pavilion — by week and hour) and again from the
   Class Builder header's Shared Facilities button. Expected: both open the
   same booking; a booking made in one shows in the other; a double-book of
   the same slot is refused with a message.
3. **Snack List (phone).** Post a snack spot for a co-op day. Then View-As
   a family in this grove: the spot appears under the kid's class on Kid
   Schedule, claim it, and it lands on their Packing List. Expected: the
   liaison sees the claim with the family name.
4. **Class Builder, scoped.** Co-op Management → Class Builder. Expected:
   she sees only her grove's morning lane (not other groves, not the
   afternoon inbox); the "Set your group's morning class" To Do shows only
   when a session is within two weeks and her grove has nothing placed.
5. **Classmates.** View-As a family in this grove → Kid Schedule → click
   the class row. Expected: the classmates list equals the My Grove roster
   (same kids, same count, own kids included).

## Exploratory charter (~10 min)

Hunt roster drift: Grove Builder placements (Membership's tool — View-As
membership@) vs My Grove vs classmates vs Directory. Pre-finalize state
must say "Placements aren't finalized yet." Try a grove with zero kids, a
kid with no photo consent (⛔ No Photos), a kid whose family has two
logins. Then open Supply Closet Inventory (Resources): she can search and
"Report low" on an item, but gets no ⚙ Storage Locations / edit controls
(those are the Supply Coordinator's).

## UX-notes lens

Anything that makes her scroll past four other sessions to find this week;
"Teaching: TBD" without a doorway to fix it; snack/facility flows that
don't confirm what got saved.
