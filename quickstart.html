New Member Quick Start

Four steps to get connected — start with step 1

Welcome to Roots & Wings! Everything we use — the members website, our chat spaces, the calendar, and shared files — runs on your new Roots & Wings Google account, so step 1 has to happen first. The rest can go in any order.

1

Activate your Roots & Wings Google account

Do this first — nothing else works until it's done.

When your family joins, our Communications Director creates a @rootsandwingsindy.com Google account for you. Google sends an invitation to your personal email address:

  1. Find the email from Google and click its Sign in button.
  2. Set your password — that's it, your account is active.
  3. Right away, add a recovery phone number: go to myaccount.google.com/security (signed in as your new account) and add your phone under Recovery phone. Brand-new accounts have no way to prove it's really you, so Google's security checks can lock you out of your next sign-in — a recovery phone means Google just texts you a code instead.
  4. Heads up: the invitation link expires after 48 hours. If yours has expired, just email communications@rootsandwingsindy.com and we'll send a fresh one.

Troubleshooting

  • Can't find the invitation email? Check your Spam and Promotions folders, and search your inbox for "Google". Still nothing — email communications@ and we'll re-send it.
  • Link expired? Email communications@ for a new invitation.
  • Google says "Verify it's you" and there's no way past it? New accounts sometimes trip Google's suspicious-sign-in check before a recovery phone is on file. Email communications@ — we can lift the check for 10 minutes while you sign in. Adding a recovery phone (step 3 above) keeps it from happening again.
  • Forgot your password later on? The Communications Director can reset it for you.
2

Join us on Google Chat

Google Chat is where all day-to-day co-op communication happens, organized into Spaces (group conversations by topic). Our Google Chat Guide describes each Space.

  • On the web: go to chat.google.com signed in with your @rootsandwingsindy.com account.
  • On your phone: install the Google Chat app (App Store / Google Play) and sign in with your Roots & Wings account.
  • Add a profile photo — it helps other members put a face to your name.

Troubleshooting

  • Don't see any Spaces? You're almost certainly signed in with your personal Gmail. Check the profile picture in the top-right corner and switch to your @rootsandwingsindy.com account.
  • Phone app only shows your personal account? Tap your profile picture → Add another account, then sign in with your Roots & Wings account and switch to it.
  • Missing messages? Open a Space → tap its name → check the notification setting isn't muted.
3

Get the co-op calendar

Co-op days, field trips, and events all live on our shared Google Calendar. The easiest view is the Calendar inside the Member Portal (step 4) — it's always up to date. To see co-op events alongside your own family calendar:

  1. Open the portal's Calendar and tap Add to Google Calendar at the bottom.
  2. Make sure Google is using your @rootsandwingsindy.com account when it opens, then confirm.

Even though the subscription lives on your Roots & Wings account, the Google Calendar app shows all your accounts' calendars in one combined view — co-op events appear right alongside your family's own appointments.

Troubleshooting

  • Button didn't work, or "Could not find calendar"? Add it manually — on a computer, open calendar.google.com (signed in as your Roots & Wings account), find Other calendars in the left sidebar, click the +, choose Subscribe to calendar, and paste this address: c_fdc0b20caba65262b9aac95ac1df638ab892fcdf1ee1ad79a1880dcc2a95b291@group.calendar.google.com
  • Subscribed on the computer but it's not on your phone? Open the Google Calendar app → Settings → tap the Roots & Wings calendar → turn on Sync.
  • Use Apple Calendar instead of the Google app? Add your Roots & Wings Google account to your iPhone under Settings → Apps → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Google — once you've subscribed to the co-op calendar (steps above), it syncs into Apple Calendar too.
4

Sign in to the Member Portal

The Member Portal at www.rootsandwingsindy.com is your home base: the family directory, schedules and rosters, the calendar, waivers, financials, and ways to get involved.

  1. Open the Members page and tap Sign in with Google.
  2. Choose your @rootsandwingsindy.com account. If your device offers your personal Gmail, tap Use another account.
  3. Once you're in, open Resources → Install the App to put the portal on your phone's home screen. You'll stay signed in for about 30 days at a time.

Troubleshooting

  • "That Google account isn't a Roots & Wings account"? You picked your personal Gmail — sign in again and choose the @rootsandwingsindy.com address.
  • Can't sign in at all? Your Google account may not be activated yet — go back to step 1.
  • Page looks stale or half-loaded? Sign out, refresh the page, and sign back in.

Stuck on anything?

Email communications@rootsandwingsindy.com for account and tech questions, or membership@rootsandwingsindy.com for everything else. We're glad you're here!
